About this model

The physical controller runs against simulated wheels, encoders and top IR inputs. The Reference profile uses the earlier approximate IR curve. Uneven wheels adds 6% right-wheel slip; Measured front IR changes only the front response using your recorded measurements. The robot follows a right-hand wall, confirms an opening, centres using odometry, turns and drives through. Escape mode has no blanket low-IR stop. The front reading still guides corner turns and checks clearance before and during exit. The arena checks the whole body for wall or doorpost contact independently. The box is 400 × 350 mm, the centred opening is 150 mm and the robot is 100 mm across. The rear IR curves and wheel response are model assumptions; verify them, wheel slip and stopping distance on the real robot. This page cannot drive the real robot.
